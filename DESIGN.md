## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, contemplative, and sincere Islamic aesthetic. Designed specifically for intentional educational platforms, it combines traditional scholarly dignity with the restraint of modern humanist typography and quiet architectural layouts.

### Emotional Tone & Brand Demeanor
- **Reverent and Grounded:** Avoids fast-paced tech clichés, synthetic gradients, hype cycles, or gamified tokens. The interface breathes with organic pacing and quiet confidence.
- **Academic and Serene:** Evokes the tactile peace of warm archival paper, fine binding, hand-crafted illumination, and natural earth pigments.
- **Radical Honesty:** Rejects artificial marketing flourishes, false counters, and hyperbolic promises. The visual voice is poised, measured, and authentic.

### Visual Architecture
The style pairs high-editorial serif typography with structured, generous negative space. Islamic architectural heritage is referenced not through clichéd dome silhouettes or heavy ornamental borders, but via delicate 8-point geometric rosettes, hairline rules in muted gold, disciplined proportional grids, and soft, tactile parchment-like surface transitions.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical manuscript illumination and natural botanical dyes. 

### Roles & Semantic Application
- **Deep Emerald (`#145B46`):** The primary color. Represents growth, vitality, and traditional Islamic scholarship. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and high-emphasis visual anchors.
- **Forest Green (`#103D32`):** The secondary color. Grounded, deep, and authoritative. Applied to high-contrast hero modules, dark footer foundations, and key section titles.
- **Muted Gold (`#C6A66B`):** The tertiary color. Sourced from illuminated Qur'anic gilded margins. Used strictly as an accent: fine ornamental rules, quote borders, insignia accents, and subtle badges. Never used for long-form reading text to preserve absolute legibility.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`):** The primary neutral canvas. Replaces clinical digital white with an organic, unbleached cotton paper tone that prevents eye strain during reflective reading.
- **Soft Sage (`#E4EDE4`):** A secondary tonal background wash. Used to group secondary card decks, highlighted callouts, and modular asides without creating harsh visual borders.
- **Text Hierarchy:** High-contrast charcoal green (`#202B26`) serves as the primary reading ink, balanced by muted slate-lichen (`#66716A`) for structural metadata, subtitles, and secondary navigation items.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a balanced dialogue between contemplative editorial serif titles and crisp, functional sans-serif body copy.

### Architectural Rules
- **Editorial Headings:** `Playfair Display` carries an intellectual, literary cadence reminiscent of classical manuscripts and fine printing. Titles prioritize generous line heights to preserve breathing room around ascenders and descenders.
- **Reading & Interface:** `Inter` handles all body text, navigational elements, and utility microcopy. It provides high legibility at all densities, contrasting harmoniously with the serif headings.
- **Sacred / Arabic Inscriptions:** Arabic passages (such as Quranic verses and du'a) demand high-clarity Naskh calligraphy rendering via dedicated fonts like `Noto Naskh Arabic` or `Amiri`. Arabic script must always be rendered with native bidirectional isolation (`dir="rtl"`), increased line height (minimum 1.8x - 2.0x), and standard non-condensed kerning.
- **Eyebrows:** Section markers use `label-eyebrow` in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`), tinted in primary emerald or muted gold to establish context before long-form titles.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional, fixed-center editorial grid capped at a maximum content width of `1200px`. Spacing is generous, prioritizing clarity and mindfulness over visual density.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margins are `1.25rem` (`20px`). Sections use `space-2xl` (`4rem`) vertical separation. Interactive cards and split narratives stack vertically into unified single-column scrolls.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid. Outer margins expand to `2.5rem` (`40px`), with gutters at `1.5rem`. Value and feature modules align into 2x2 grids.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid. Outer canvas margins expand to `4rem` (`64px`), centered with `gutter-desktop` (`2rem`). Major section paddings expand to `space-3xl` (`6rem`) to ensure contemplative whitespace between thematic shifts.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids high-contrast drop shadows and artificial 3D lighting. Depth is rendered primarily through tonal layering and low-contrast surface outlines.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** The base layer uses Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`).
- **Mid Tier (Section Accents):** Soft Sage (`#E4EDE4`) defines thematic structural bands, anchored seamlessly without floating effects.
- **Top Tier (Interactive Panels):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards sit atop the canvas, framed by a delicate border (`1px solid #E7E5DC`).

### Ambient Shadows
Where elevation is necessary to convey interactivity or focus, the system relies on warm, tinted micro-shadows:
- **Default Card Elevation:** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 61, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(16, 61, 50, 0.02)`.
- **Hover / Active Lift:** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 61, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 61, 50, 0.03)`. The shadow is tinted with the forest-green base rather than cold synthetic black.
- **Verse Panels & Holy Text Containers:** No floating shadows. Grounded with a double hairline border (outer: `#E7E5DC`, inner inset: `#C6A66B` at 30% opacity).

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, welcoming curvature combined with subtle classical proportions. 

### Curvature Tokens
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) provides tactile definition.
- **Standard Surfaces (Cards, Callouts):** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) creates inviting, organic cards that feel approachable for all generations.
- **Hero Banners & Focal Panels:** `rounded-xl` (`1.25rem` – `1.5rem` / `20px` – `24px`) soften dominant page landmarks.
- **Pill Accents:** Reserved strictly for badge pills and small eyebrow markers (`9999px`).

### Islamic Motifs & Ornamentation
- Pure geometric watermarks (such as the 8-point rosette / Khatam) may be used as background accents with ultra-low opacity (between `3%` and `6%`).
- Dividers feature an optional center diamond or geometric dot accent rendered in Muted Gold.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Emerald (`#145B46`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg`, with internal padding `12px 24px`. Subtle transition on hover to Forest Green (`#103D32`). Focus state displays a `2px` offset ring in Muted Gold (`#C6A66B`).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent surface with a `1.5px` border in Deep Emerald (`#145B46`) and matching label color. On hover, fills with Soft Sage (`#E4EDE4` at 40% opacity).
- **Text Anchor:** Clean sans-serif link in Deep Emerald with an underline offset of `4px` in Muted Gold.

### Cards
- **Content Card:** Built on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border in `#E7E5DC` and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners. Padding is `space-xl` (`24px` to `32px`). Cards lift subtly on hover (`-2px` transform) with an ambient tinted shadow.
- **Dark Inverted Banner:** Grounded in Forest Green (`#103D32`), Warm Ivory typography, subtle watermark rosettes in the corner, and an accent CTA button in Muted Gold or Pure White.

### Quranic Verse Panel
- Dedicated showcase component. Features a Warm Ivory or Pure White background encased by a `1px` border in `#E7E5DC` with an interior accent border in Muted Gold (`#C6A66B`).
- Arabic text is centered, formatted with `dir="rtl"`, rendered at large scale (`quran-verse` token), and separated from the translation by an ornamental gold dividing motif. Translation and surah attribution sit underneath in balanced, understated sans-serif hierarchy.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pure White background with an `#E7E5DC` border, `rounded-md`, and primary text color. Focus ring is Deep Emerald (`#145B46`) with zero offset blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Styled in Deep Emerald with smooth SVG checkmark glyphs in Pure White. Unchecked state is a clean neutral border on ivory.

### Badges & Eyebrow Chips
- Subtle pill shapes with Soft Sage (`#E4EDE4`) fill and Deep Emerald (`#145B46`) text, or Warm Ivory fill with a `1px` Muted Gold border for spiritual attributions and chapter headings.
